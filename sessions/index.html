The session lineup for the 2026 Advanced Training Design Workshop.

2026 lineup / Dec 4-5 / Sixteen sessions

Sixteen sessions across two days, all in the same room. Every one of them runs the same way: about 25 minutes of hands-on demonstration, 15 minutes of what and why, and 10 minutes of the room pushing back.

Filter by category below, or see the running order on the schedule.

A few details are still being confirmed with presenters, and the Saturday 4:00 slot is an ALERRT Center session with the topic to come. Presenters: send materials you want attendees to have to sd33@txstate.edu and they will be linked from your session page.
